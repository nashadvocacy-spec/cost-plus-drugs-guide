Cost Plus Drugs (full name: Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drug Company) is an online pharmacy. It mails prescription medicines to your home. It shows you exactly how each price is figured, so there are no surprises.

Good to know

  • It is mail order. Medicines come in the mail, not from a store counter.
  • You do not need insurance to use it. You can pay the posted price.
  • It works with some insurance plans, but not all.
  • Your doctor still has to send a prescription.
  • You must be 18 or older to make an account.
If you have Medicare drug coverage (Part D or a Medicare Advantage plan with drug coverage): a lower price is not always a lower total. Sometimes your plan copay is cheaper. And if you pay cash outside your plan, ask your plan whether that purchase counts toward your deductible and yearly out-of-pocket limit. Step 2 and Step 3 help you check both.

Have your pill bottles or your medicine list nearby. For each medicine, write down what you pay now, then look up the Cost Plus price.

  1. Open costplusdrugs.com (opens in a new window).
  2. Type the medicine name in the search box. Generic names work best (for example, "atorvastatin" instead of "Lipitor").
  3. Pick the same strength (like 20 mg) and the same number of pills you get now.
  4. Write the price in the form below.

Not every medicine is sold there. If you cannot find one, write "not listed" and keep getting it where you do now.

Then

  1. Click the button to sign up or create an account.
  2. Fill in your information. Go slowly, and double-check your address.
  3. If you have insurance, add it now or later in your account settings.
  4. Write your login email below. Keep your password somewhere safe, not on this page.
Family helpers: it is fine to sit beside your loved one and help. The account should be in the name of the person taking the medicine, with their own information.

Cost Plus cannot fill anything until your doctor sends the prescription to them. Call the office, send a patient portal message, or ask at your next visit.

What to say

"Please send my prescription for ____ electronically to Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drug Company. It is a mail-order pharmacy."

Good questions to ask

Do not stop or change any medicine because of price without talking with your doctor or pharmacist first.
  1. Watch your email. Cost Plus will let you know when they receive the prescription.
  2. Log in to your account and review your order and the price.
  3. If you added insurance, you will see your insurance price at checkout. Compare it with the cash price.
  4. Pay and choose shipping.
  5. Watch for your package in the mail.
Do not wait until your last pill. Mail takes time. Keep using your current pharmacy until your first order arrives safely. Start this process when you still have at least two to three weeks of medicine left.
